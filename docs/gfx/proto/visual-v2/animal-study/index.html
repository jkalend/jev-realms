Wolf

Archetype: Wolf • Form: wolf • Scale: 0.90 • Role: Beast
Review & Enhancements:
• Topline & Posture: Replaced horizontal tube body with distinct canid anatomy: high muscular withers over the shoulders (Y=1.30), deep ribcage, and steep abdominal tuck.
• Color Zoning & Ruff: Added ventral pale cream throat/chest bib and flaring cheek tufts. Replaced isolated rib fins with dorsal shoulder hackles.
• Skull & Ears: Chiseled lupine muzzle with distinct brow stop, pale lower jaw, and alert triangular ears lined with pale inner fur.
• Brush Tail: Replaced stiff upright flagpole with low-slung, bushy fur tail that curves naturally behind the rear with a dark tip.
• Motion: Attack animation now lunges forward with coiled leg drive instead of freezing the limbs.

Bear

Archetype: Bear • Form: bear • Scale: 1.30 • Role: Beast (Apex Predator)
Review & Enhancements:
• Massive Grizzly Shoulder Hump: The defining grizzly hallmark: towering muscular shoulder crest rising to Y=1.48 behind the neck, eliminating the previous hippo-like profile.
• Facial Character: Broad cranial vault with wide dish stop, heavy blunt honey-tan snout mask, and dark leathery nose leather.
• Ear Cups: Replaced vertical top spheres with natural cupped rounded ears set wide on the lateral skull with interior shadow pockets.
• Vicious Claws: Added 5 prominent curved bone-colored digging claws jutting forward on each massive front paw.
• Chest Bib: Warm golden-brown crescent bib across the massive barrel chest.

Rat

Archetype: Rat • Form: rat • Scale: 0.72 • Role: Beast (Cellar Vermin)
Review & Enhancements:
• Hunched Kyphotic Silhouette: Replaced long straight cylinder with authentic rodent posture: low scuttling shoulders, high arched hunchback (Y=1.14), and low tucked rump.
• Flexible Sinuous Tail: Curves naturally from low pelvis, sweeps near the floor, and coils upward at the tip.
• Rodent Head: Forward wedge skull with rounded vibrissal pads, pink nose tip, and lower incisor shelf.
• Ears & Paws: Translucent rounded pink saucer ears and dexterous pink paws replacing the stiff upright spoons.
