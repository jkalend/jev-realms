Scope: Diablo II: Lord of Destruction boss fights for adaptation into Laya Realms. Sources: Arreat Summit (official Blizzard classic.battle.net archive), Diablo Wiki (Fandom). Stats given as Normal/Nightmare/Hell where relevant. This file is data-dense notes, not prose.

Super Unique rules (global): fixed spawn location every game; gain +1 magical bonus in Nightmare, +2 in Hell; cannot be frozen (chilled only); Crushing Blow damage halved vs them; do not regenerate HP [20].

## 3. Boss-Supporting Systems

### 3.1 Immunities per difficulty
- Rule: Immune = resistance ≥100%. "A few monsters in Normal... In Nightmare Difficulty, more monsters have Immunities. In Hell Difficulty almost all Monsters are immune to something." [25]
- Concrete immunity instances from boss tables: Rakanishu Fire Immune Hell (FR 110) [23]; Talic Fire Immune Hell (110) [24]; Madawc Lightning Immune Hell (LR 100) [24]; Korlic Cold Immune Hell (CR 150) [24]; Corpsefire Cold Immune Hell (120) [21]; Ghostly Champions = DR 80% (displayed "Immune to Physical") [26].
- Build-diversity consequence: melee must handle Phys-Immune via elemental skills (Paladin Vengeance, Barbarian Berserk), hirelings, or elemental charms, per official tips [25]. Sorceress etiquette: her whole identity is one-element-per-tree (Fire/Lightning/Cold skill pages) — a mono-element build literally cannot damage some Hell packs; solutions are Cold/Lightning dual-spec, merc damage (Act II merc), or party synergy. (Analysis grounded in [25] + skill tree structure.)
- Unique scaling: +1 bonus ability in NM, +2 in Hell [25]; resist bonuses never create a third immunity [26].

### 3.2 Unique/Champion affix pool (the actual "affix table" bosses borrow from)
Champion variants: Champion (+90/75/66% dmg), Ghostly (DR 80 + cold dmg), Fanatic (+100 move speed, -70% def), Berserker (+270% dmg taken-as-glass), Possessed (2× life, uncurseable) [26]. Unique modifiers [26]:
| Affix | Mechanic |
|---|---|
| Lightning Enchanted | 8 Charged Bolts (lvl mlvl/2) per hit/hit-recovery taken |
| Fire Enchanted | On-death explosion, phys+fire, % of max base monster life |
| Cold Enchanted | Cold dmg + chill length 4s+0.2/lvl; death novas |
| Multishot | Triples missile count |
| Aura Enchanted | Might/Holy Fire/Blessed Aim/Holy Freeze/Holy Shock/Conviction/Fanaticism (aura level = mlvl/6 or /8) |
| Cursed | 75% chance to Amplify Damage on its hits |
| Extra Fast / Extra Strong | +100% move speed (pack-wide) / +135% dmg |
| Mana Burn | +66–100% mana damage — bug: ×256 multiplier from melee hits |
| Teleport | Ports when <30% HP (and heals some) |
| Spectral Hit / Stone Skin / Magic Resistant | +res per element / +50% DR, ×2 def / +40% C-F-L res |

Boss usage: Talic ships with Fire Enchanted [24]; Rakanishu with Lightning Enchanted + Extra Fast [23]; Blood Raven with Spectral Hit + Cursed [22]; Hephasto fixed with Conviction + Spectral Hit + Magic Resistant [8]. Random uniques get the table stuff above.

### 3.3 Potion / rejuvenation economy during boss fights
- **Healing potions heal over time, never instant**: 30–640 HP depending on tier+class (Barb gets 2× Sorc per bottle); drinking multiple stacks the total restored, not the rate; wasting is possible — so you must *disengage* to let ticks land ("best to run away from combat long enough for them to heal fully") [16].
- **Rejuvenation potions are the panic button**: instant 35% life+mana; Full Rejuv instant 100%. Advice: keep for "desperate situations" [16].
- **Counter-potions as fight prep**: Antidote = cure poison + **+50 PR & +10 max PR for 30 s**; Thawing = cure chill + +50 CR / +10 max CR / 30 s; durations stack when pre-gulped (Duriel prep) [16][15]. Stamina potions = guaranteed escape fuel (needed to keep evading Diablo's hose; running out while low-stamina = death) [12][16].
- **Mercs drink too**: Shift+1..4 feeds potions to hirelings — the core Duriel/Andariel merc-tank loop [16].
- Economy: vendors refill potions/ID/TP scrolls; shift-click buys auto-fill the belt — creates the "restock run" cadence between boss attempts [16].

### 3.4 Monster regeneration → the Town-Portal reset dynamic
- "On higher difficulty levels, Monsters regenerate health very quickly... notice his health quickly regenerates if you don't attack him" [25]. Prevent Monster Heal exists specifically to counter it [25]; Super Uniques uniquely "do not regenerate damage" [20].
- TP loop (officially taught in every boss tip): open a Town Portal BEFORE pulling (Andariel/Duriel tips), die → corpse-run through the portal chain, or bail early to restock potions — but every second withdrawn is boss HP ticked back on Nightmare/Hell [25][1][2]. Uber Diablo explicitly regens to full ~30 s into combat if DPS is low → function-requiring PMH/Open Wounds [12].
- Psychological design: TP = free bail option ("when in trouble, run" is official guidance) [25]; bosses punish greedy restocking with regen; Diablo punishes fleeing TOO close by Bone-Prisoning your portal [12][4].

## 4. Design Analysis — transferable patterns for Laya Realms

| Pattern | D2 exemplar (cited) | Adaptation for a small tactical roguelike |
|---|---|---|
| **One readable signature move per fight** | Blizzard keeps it learnable by name: "Red Lightning Hose" [4][12], "Mooshield... Poison Spray" [10], Hoarfrost knockback wedge [5], Rakanishu's per-hit Charged Bolts [23] | Give each boss exactly one named, telegraphed ability (an arm-raise windup like Diablo's hose [12] translates cleanly to a 1-turn charge-up telegraph in turn-based combat). Everything else is a supporting cast move. |
| **Checks that scale with progression** | poison res (Act I) → gear/cold res (Act II Duriel) → unleechable resist wall (Act III Mephisto) → multi-element resist + dodge (Act IV Diablo) → attrition/resource (Act V Baal) [1][2][3][4][5]; difficulty layers immunities [25] | Escalate in this order: single-defense check → defense+consumable check → build-integrity check → movement/telegraph check → economy check. A check the player already solved should re-appear as a normal elite affix later (Lightning Enchanted does exactly this [26]). |
| **Phases that change movement rules** | Diablo's arena tells: hoses force circling; Bone Prison deletes your escape portal; his leash creates safe halls [12]. Baal: Hoarfrost pushes + Festering Appendage blocks retreat + Vile Effigy splits targets [5]. The Ancients: no retreat at all (TP = full reset) [14] | In turn-based terms: phase transitions that (a) add zones (fire ring cells), (b) invert mobility (slow aura ring follows boss [2]), or (c) lock retreat as a late-game boss trait. Ancients-style "no bail" is a great late-roguelike twist because earlier bosses trained TP-freedom [14]. |
| **Adds as resource drains, not DPS meters** | Baal's whole P1 is five named packs before him [13][14]; Mephisto's Council must be cleared or cheated away [3]; Blood Raven's Hungry Dead force potion/attention spend [22]; Nihlathak's minions ARE his ammunition (corpse explosion can delete your own army... and you) [9] | Adds should consume a specific player budget (potions, cooldowns, positioning), and one boss should invert the rules: player corpses/kills feed the boss weapon (Nihlathak [9]), forcing counter-play (corpse denial, freeze-shatter, "Rest in Peace"). |
| **Pre-fight economy as the real boss mechanic** | Rejuvs as panic buttons, thawing/antidote 30-s pre-buffs, merc feeding, TP restock vs boss regen [16][25] | Make the fight decision start one room earlier: "what 3 consumables do I walk in with?" D2's discipline of healing-over-time vs instant rejuv (slow vs burst healing) maps to turn-based action-economy (use-an-item forgoes an attack) [16]. |
| **Reward the fight with a system unlock, not just loot** | The Smith → free Imbue (learns that "craft" exists) [6][17]; Hephasto gates the Hellforge rune crush [8]; Baal gates the next difficulty [14] | Boss death = unlock a crafting/talent tier so the fight teaches a system the rest of the game then exercises. |

Caveat worth carrying: Duriel's legendary difficulty is largely *circumstance* — smallest room + lowest player power point + no escape — not raw stats [15]. Ambience of constraint beats numbers; Laya Realms can manufacture this deliberately with a tight arena + TP disable on one "wall" boss.
